## 4. Antigravity 的双档配额与超额切换（Pro 页原文）

```text
**Increased AI Agent Quotas:** Google AI Pro members and trial memberships from eligible premium device purchases receive higher usage limits for advanced models within Google Antigravity.
```

```text
- **Baseline quota:** Based on your subscription tier, you receive time-bound usage limits specific to Google Antigravity. [Learn more about Google Antigravity](https://antigravity.google/docs/plans).
- **AI credits:** When you use up your baseline quota, you can purchase AI credits through Google One to continue usage. These credits can be used across supported Google products. AI credits are deducted based on standard API pricing for the specific model and complexity of request.
```

```text
- **Toggle for overage:** You can manage extra usage by turning on a toggle to use AI credits for overage when base limits are reached.
- **Never:** When you reach your baseline quota, you'll find a "Baseline model quota reached" notification.
- **Always:** The moment your baseline quota ends, Antigravity automatically uses your AI credit balance.
```

```text
To use Google Antigravity, you must be 18 years or older.
```

```text
Only English is supported for prompts and instructions.
```

```text
Available capacity may vary and isn't guaranteed.
```

```text
This feature is available as a desktop app for Windows, macOS, and Linux.
```

Gemini Apps 侧同一机制的措辞：`**Toggle off (default):** When you run out of your base quota, you'll find a "Limit reached" notification.` / `**Toggle on**: Gemini automatically uses your AI credit balance when you run out of your base quota.`；设置入口 `the "Settings" then "Usage limits"`。

## 11. 本次采集发现的三处官方表述差异

1. **Tasks in Gemini**：套餐页把 `Tasks in Gemini` 写成 Ultra 的 `first access to advanced features`；帮助页 14534406 给出实际范围——在 Australia、Canada、India、Japan、South Korea、US 无需任何订阅即可使用，其他地区需 Pro 或 Ultra，且 EEA、Nigeria、Switzerland、United Kingdom 不可用。以帮助页为准。
2. **模型命名滞后**：套餐页 FAQ 仍写 `3.1 Pro` 与 `Veo 3.1 Lite`，同页套餐卡片已是 Gemini 4 系列（Neon / Hydrogen / Carbon）与 Gemini Omni Flash。
3. **Photos 可用地区**：Pro 页写 `currently available in select countries on Android devices, iPhone, and iPad`，Plus 页写 `currently available in the US on Android devices, iPhone, and iPad`。
